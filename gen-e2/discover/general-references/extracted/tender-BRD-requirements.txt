2. Introduction

NTUC Club is seeking a vendor to configure, integrate and implement a loyalty programme technology
platform for its portfolio of leisure, recreation and lifestyle destinations ("the Club's touchpoint"), with all
business units (including Downtown East (DE), D’Resort, MICE, NTUC Club Clubhouses, Orchid Country Club
(OCC), Aranda Country Club (ACC), Wild Wild Wet (WWW), My Golf Kaki (MGK), D’Resort), and its
participating tenants. The programme is intended to capture first-party member data, change customer
behaviour in ways that grow revenue and margin, and channel value to participating tenants.

The programme will be delivered in three phases:

  • Phase 1 — MVP: a simple, free-to-join, two-tier points programme rolled out to participating tenants at
    the 4 NTUC Club Clubhouses, Orchid Country Club, Aranda Country Club and Downtown East (DE). Scope
    covers participating tenants at these locations only and excludes business units. Targeted Go-Live: 1
    December 2026.
  • Phase 2 — Growth & Engagement: deeper systems integration (parking, POS, other facilities),
    missions/challenges, and expansion of tiering and partner offers, covering all business units (including
    Downtown East (DE), D’Resort, MICE, NTUC Club Clubhouses, Orchid Country Club (OCC), Aranda
    Country Club (ACC), Wild Wild Wet (WWW), My Golf Kaki (MGK), D’Resort), and its participating tenants.
  • Phase 3 — Mature Ecosystem: advanced personalisation, gamification, an experience marketplace,
    member community features and referral mechanics.
NTUC Club is not prescribing the underlying platform or architecture. Vendors are asked to propose a solution
— white-label or otherwise — that provides a stable foundation of core member benefits at launch while
remaining highly configurable for evolving marketing benefits, without requiring major platform
redevelopment as the programme matures.

Proposals should include a 3-year roadmap, timeline and cost breakdown covering the Phase 1, together with
ongoing support and enhancement costs. For Phase 2 and 3, a budgetary estimate is required based on 3
years of operation.



3. Business Background

3.1 Why This Programme Is Needed
  • Capture first-party member data to better serve NTUC members.
  • Change customer behaviour in ways that drive revenue and margin.
  • Give greater value back to participating tenants.

3.2 Key Customer Segments
Families, PMEs (Professionals, Managers and Executives), and DINKs/Silvers (dual-income-no-kids and
senior/retiree households).

3.3 Customer Proposition
Brand promise: “Your Third Place Rewards You More”

Your favourite place to eat, play and connect — the more you visit, the more rewarding every visit becomes.
Members earn points, enjoy member-exclusive perks, and unlock greater benefits over time. Tagline direction

accelerators (e.g. bonus value for multiple same-day receipts across different tenants) and campaign-driven

3.4 Expected Programme Outcomes
  • Allow NTUC Club to track every visitor across every touchpoint.
  • Enable NTUC Club to channel customers to tenants, including its own businesses.

4. Project Objectives

  • Deliver a branded loyalty platform under the NTUC Club identity, whether through a white-label or
    equivalent configurable solution that is easy to use for members and administrators, and that can scale
    and grow with the programme as it develops.
  • Deliver a stable foundation of core member benefits at launch, while enabling highly configurable,
    evolving marketing benefits that adapt to changing customer needs, business priorities and market
    conditions — without requiring major platform redevelopment.
  • Deliver the approved Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to Go-Live by 1 December 2026, covering its
    participating tenants in the 4 NTUC Club Clubhouses, OCC, ACC and Downtown East (DE) – Phase 1.
  • This programme should eventually cover all business units (including Downtown East (DE), D’Resort,
    MICE, NTUC Club Clubhouses, Orchid Country Club (OCC), Aranda Country Club (ACC), Wild Wild Wet
    (WWW), My Golf Kaki (MGK), D’Resort), and its participating tenants – Phase 2 and Phase 3.
  • Establish a single loyalty programme framework that can be adopted across all NTUC Club business units
    and its participating tenants.
  • Vendors should recommend their preferred approach for delivering the solution as a mobile application,
    noting any implications for integration. The MVP scope has been properly defined in this document and
    is expected to be delivered in full, ideally benchmarked against comparable programmes (e.g. Changi
    Rewards App) in terms of usability and feature maturity. Where Vendors see value in additional
    capabilities beyond this scope, these may be proposed separately for discussion.

11. Tier Structure & Rebate Yield Scenarios

The tier structure below reflects the current three-tier design (One / Two – NTUC Member / Three – High-
Value Customer), all earning at $1 spend = 1 point. Tier 3 is scoped to launch in Phase 2. NTUC Club is
evaluating two rebate-yield scenarios; the redemption value per 1,000 points differs between them, but earn
mechanics and tier structure are otherwise identical. The final scenario adopted will directly drive the
platform's redemption conversion table and cost/liability model, so it is provided here for vendor cost-
modelling purposes.

Reward points are valid for 1 year from the date earned and expire automatically thereafter. To maintain Tier

17. Assumptions, Dependencies & Constraints

17.1 Assumptions
  • The programme is organisation-wide in intent (not limited to Downtown East (DE)), with Phase 1
    onboarding DE tenants first.
  • SingPass/MyInfo will be the baseline identity and data-capture mechanism for member enrolment,
    consistent with current NTUC standards.
  • NTUC Club will provide the funding policy, final tier qualification criteria and rebate-yield scenario
    decision in time to inform solution design sign-off.
  • Existing member/transaction data (where it exists) will be made available by NTUC HQ systems for
    migration in a reasonable format.

17.2 Dependencies
  • Existing Parking Integration vendor (Wei Long) cooperation for Phase 2 carpark system integration.
  • Tenant participation agreements (opt-in basis, POS/receipt-capture cooperation) being in place ahead of
    each phase.
  • SNDGO/GovTech approval and API access for SingPass/MyInfo integration.

17.3 Constraints
  • Phase 1 MVP must go live by 1 December 2026.



                                                        Page 21 of 22
NTUC Club — Loyalty Programme BRD                                                   Confidential — Tender Use Only



  • Managing bespoke POS integration at every individual tenant is not operationally viable; Phase 2 capture
    approach must be scalable across many tenants.
  • Budget and resourcing for a 3-year phased roadmap, to be confirmed through the vendor cost proposal
    and internal budget approval.
  • Member, transaction and behavioural data shall remain the property of NTUC Club and must be
    accessible through standard integration and export mechanisms.
  • The solution shall support bi-directional integration with NTUC's CRM systems for unified member
    profiles, consent management, campaign orchestration and member behaviour insights.
  • The solution must comply with applicable PDPA and NTUC Club information security requirements.
