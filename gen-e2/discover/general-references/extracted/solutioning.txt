(API, webhooks, admin dashboard), creating a seamless launch experience for users.

This is simple by design to hit the 1 Dec launch date – a free, two-tier points programme before future scaling.

Tenant Solution & Onboarding
• Scan with any mobile device
     o For phase 1, the tenants will be able to login to
       the tenant app through any device, scan the
       loyalty id, then input the transaction amount in
       order to complete a transaction.

• Hardware Integration (Phase 2 – For exploration)
     o Hardware solutions will be explored to record
       richer transaction data such as SKUs,
       transaction ID and other details coming from
       the POS

• Training
      o Digital training materials will be provided to
        each tenant in order to educate staff in
        performing earn, redemption, reversal and
        offline fallback

• App Access
     o Every new tenant will be provided access to the
       tenant app, where they can administer earn and
       redeem transactions
