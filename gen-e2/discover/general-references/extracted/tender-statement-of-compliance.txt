The Tenderer shall fill in the Statement of Compliance Table with one of the following
responses for all clauses in Part 2 – Requirement Specification:

         “Compliance”                                   or                              “C”
          : Able to comply fully with the requirements. The Tenderer shall not add comments
         against the clause that vary the meaning of full compliance. However, comments
         indicating references to supporting documentation or literature to substantiate the
         response are permissible. Any other comments which vary the meaning of full
         compliance will be ignored.

                For statements that do not require the Tenderer to meet a specific requirement
                but merely provide information, the Tenderer's response shall state “C”.

         “Partially                    Compliant”                    or                     “PC”
          : Able to comply with the requirements only in part. The Tenderer shall clearly identify
         the aspects of the requirement that can be met and those that cannot, together with any
         proposed alternatives, workarounds, or future enhancements.

                An explanatory note must be provided under the "Remarks" column for all
                responses marked "PC".

         “Non-Compliant”                                     or                              “NC”
          : Unable to comply with the requirements.

                An explanatory note must be provided under the "Remarks" column for all
                responses marked "NC", stating the reason for non-compliance and, where
                applicable, any alternative solution that may be offered.

         “Not                     Applicable”                     or                     “NA”
          : The requirement is not applicable to the Tenderer's proposed solution, services, or
         scope of supply.

                An explanatory note must be provided under the "Remarks" column to justify
                why the requirement is considered not applicable.

   •     Vague responses such as "Refer to brochure attached" are not acceptable. The Tenderer
         shall provide clear, specific, and sufficiently detailed explanations to support the stated
         compliance status.
